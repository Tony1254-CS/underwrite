# UNDERWRITE — Product Film Production Brief

## Creative thesis

**Title:** Authority, Earned.

**Core line:** AI proposes. Evidence sizes its authority. Contracts enforce the boundary. A bond contains residual loss.

The film is a product demonstration, not a website tour. Actual light-mode product interaction carries the story. Motion graphics explain relationships that are difficult to see in one screen; they never replace product evidence.

## Runtime and delivery

- Final runtime: 4:40.53; hard ceiling 5:00.
- Master and delivery: H.264 MP4, 1920×1080 progressive, 30 fps, BT.709-compatible YUV420P, AAC 48 kHz.
- Narration: natural female English voice, calm and technically confident.
- Captions: burned-in English plus a separate SRT.
- Theme: light only.

## Visual language

- Warm ivory background, black typography, UNDERWRITE lime as the single energy color.
- One continuous lime “authority line” connects exam, policy, settlement, bond and revocation.
- Real product screens are flat and legible. Avoid fake browser chrome, gratuitous 3D rotation, neon effects and generic AI imagery.
- Cursor appears only for meaningful actions.
- Use hard cuts, short match cuts and restrained 8–12 frame dissolves.

## Evidence classes

Every scene that can be misunderstood carries one persistent label:

- `CONTROLLED LOCAL DEMO · NO REAL FUNDS`
- `SIMULATED ATTACK · SCRIPTED INPUT`
- `REAL BASE SEPOLIA · CONTROLLED MOCKUSDC · TIER 2`
- `CONTROLLED, NON-INDEPENDENT EVALUATION`
- `SECURITY SELF-ASSESSMENT · NOT AN AUDIT`

Real public-chain evidence uses solid green. Controlled local product state uses amber hatching. Scripted or replayed results use a gray dashed treatment.

## Editorial rule

No competition name, organizer, scoring rubric, deadline, prize, submission instruction or judging reference appears in narration, titles, captions, metadata or end cards.
