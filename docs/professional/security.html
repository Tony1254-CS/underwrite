01 · Scope and opinion

Reasonable prototype assurance, explicit residual risk

The design materially limits what a compromised model or agent key can do. Its strongest property is that critical authorization rules are deterministic and re-enforced by the vault. It remains a student research prototype with central trust assumptions and no external audit.

Assessment basis

  • Solidity and TypeScript source review
  • Named safety invariants and exact test mapping
  • Foundry unit, fuzz and stateful invariant results
  • Engine unit/integration results
  • Deployment bytecode/source publication
  • RPC receipts and decoded event evidence

Out of scope

  • Formal verification
  • Independent penetration test or audit
  • Mainnet economics and operational custody
  • Legal or regulatory opinion
  • Independent model benchmark
  • Production cloud/infrastructure hardening

Core assumptions

  • The chain consensus and ERC-20 token behavior are outside the application’s control.
  • Owner, examiner, underwriter, adjudicator and guardian keys are distinct in the demo.
  • Trusted vendor and PO records are correct; the LLM does not author them.
  • Exam distributions approximate relevant threats only within documented limits.
  • Production deployment would replace local software keys with organizational custody controls.

Secret handling rule

Keys, seeds and API credentials never belong in code, logs, prompts, evidence, documents or commits.

The deployed addresses are public evidence. Their private keys are intentionally excluded from every submission artifact and model context.

11 · Response and standards alignment

Fail safe, preserve evidence, recover deliberately

DETECT

Monitor policy reason codes, transaction failures, unusual velocity, exposure, key use and qualification changes.

CONTAIN

Pause the vault, veto pending requests, deactivate recipients, revoke/suspend qualification and stop agent services.

PRESERVE

Capture receipts, decoded logs, manifest/report hashes, configuration, timestamps and affected invoice state.

RECOVER

Rotate keys, reconcile/resolve eligible records, review root cause, rerun exam, issue a new qualification and document acceptance.
